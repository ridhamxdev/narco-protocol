# PRODUCT.md — NARCO PROTOCOL

*(Inferred from the owner's explicit brief; assumptions labeled.)*

## What it is
A single-user personal discipline dashboard ("personal operating system") the owner uses daily
while hunting for a software job. Name chosen by the owner: **NARCO PROTOCOL** — after his
nickname "Narco". Tagline: *The empire is built one day at a time.*

## The one mechanism
Every daily obligation — 10 job applications, worship, gym, oats/clean food, call-backs,
game-time limit, lights-out by 00:30, quality time — is composed into **one scored ledger per
day**. 100% = a conquered day; streaks of conquered days are the wealth.

## Audience & scene
One person (the owner), mostly on his phone: mornings planning, nights logging in bed.
Dark rooms, one thumb, short sessions. Secondary: desktop between job applications.
He gets bored by bland trackers — the surface must feel like a boss's war room, not a to-do app.

## Jobs to be done
1. Log everything for today in seconds (Operate).
2. See instantly what's still open before the day closes.
3. Feel the streak — motivation through visible accumulation.
4. Manage the rules (targets, reminder times, habits) in Settings.
5. Get nagged by email (nodemailer) when he forgets — especially missed call-backs.

## Constraints & commitments
- Stack: Next.js App Router (JS), Tailwind v4, framer-motion, JWT auth, MongoDB, single `server.js` locally, Vercel-deployable.
- Brand commitment (owner-pinned): dark premium, "cartel ledger" world — warm black, aged gold,
  condensed display numerals, mono ledger labels. Not neon, not glassmorphism, not cream/serif.
- Faces: Anton (display), Archivo (body/UI), Sometype Mono (labels/data).
- Mobile-first; bottom tab bar on phone, rail on desktop. Reduced motion respected.
- Worship is a first-class habit — treated with respect, never joked about.

## Voice
Short, imperative, self-addressed orders ("Log it. Move."). Numbers speak loudest.
No corporate softness, no cutesy gamification copy. Errors state facts + the fix.
